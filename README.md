# Rock Paper Scissors: Fighter

Fast-paced, two-player fighting game where Rock, Paper, Scissors turns into a fighter game. The game is a best of 5/first to 3 points. Sit with a friend at the keyboard and fight against each other in this unique version of Rock Paper Scissors.

## Choose Your Fighter

Play as one of three unique characters, each with distinct stats, strengths, and combat mechanics:

Rock: Deal damage by falling onto your opponent, using your weight and positioning to your advantage.

Paper: Take to the skies with reduced gravity, no jump cooldown, and extended airtime. Use your aerial mobility to control the battlefield.

Scissors: Strike with speed and precision. Double-tap left or right to dash into your opponent and deal damage.

Each character has different movement speeds, health, and damage values, making every matchup a unique challenge.

## Controls

### Player 1

Move: W, A, S, D

### Player 2

Move: Arrow keys

## Character Selection

During character selection, Player 1 goes first, then Player 2 selects their character.

Press 1 for Rock, 2 for Paper, or 3 for Scissors.

Alternatively, click on your desired character.

## Unique Abilities

### Rock:

While in the air, press down to perform a **quick fall**, dealing more damage

### Paper:

Fly up using reduced gravity and zero jump cooldown for **extended airtime**. Staying in the air forever will be impossible though.

### Scissors: 

Scissors can **perform dashes** by double-tapping the side buttons in one direction (A or D/← or →).

## Advantage System

Combat revolves around the classic Rock, Paper, Scissors advantage system. Your character's matchup against your opponent determines how many hits are generally needed to secure a victory:

Advantage: Approximately 2 hits to defeat your opponent.

Neutral: Approximately 3 hits.

Disadvantage: Approximately 4 hits.

Damage mechanics differ between characters. Rock and Scissors deal damage based on individual collisions, while Paper's damage is calculated per attack. As a result, the actual number of hits required may vary.
